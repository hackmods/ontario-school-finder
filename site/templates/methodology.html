{{ _("Student population and enrolment") }}

{{ _h("The “About the students” figures (first language, newcomers, special education, gifted, low-income households and parents’ education) come from the latest Ministry file ({sif}). The last two are estimates the Ministry builds by matching student postal codes to Statistics Canada census data. Values are suppressed (SP) when fewer than 10 students are represented. The Ministry publishes whole percentages, so 0% can mean a few students rather than none; a missing value is shown as its code (such as NA), never as 0. {{ _h("“Open at this school number since” is the date in the Ministry’s contact list for the school number. About half of all schools carry 1969-09-01, the earliest date the list uses, so we show “1969 or earlier”. It is not the age of a building: schools move, are rebuilt and are renumbered.") }}

{{ _h("Class sizes come from lists that name schools rather than numbering them. When two schools in one board share a name, the row can’t be split and neither school gets class sizes; their pages say “source match unresolved” rather than guess.") }}

{{ _("School names are the Ministry’s, with one tidy-up: a few names in its files repeat a phrase (for example “Glenwood School School” or “B Davison Secondary School Secondary School”), and we show it once.
