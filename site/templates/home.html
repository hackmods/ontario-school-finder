    What EQAO results are — and what they aren’t

    EQAO (the Education Quality and Accountability Office) gives the same reading, writing and math assessments to every Ontario student in Grades 3, 6 and 9, plus the Grade 10 literacy test (OSSLT). The number we show is the share of a school’s students who met the provincial standard.

    {{ icon("info") }}Not a measure of

    • {{ icon("x") }}How good the teachers are, or how happy and safe students feel.
    • {{ icon("x") }}Arts, sports, French, special education or the many things tests don’t cover.
    • {{ icon("x") }}A fair ranking. Small schools swing a lot from year to year, and student needs differ widely.
