# French glossary (Ontario education terms)

Use these every time, so the French version reads as one site. Use the terms Ontario's Ministry of Education,
EQAO/OQRE and French-language boards use. Canadian French, plain language, "vous", short sentences. Keep proper
names (schools, boards, cities, document titles) and numbers as they are.
